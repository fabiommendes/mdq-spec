Date: 2026-10-20

Scope: The fill-in question type: docs/question-types/fill-in.md and schema/fill-in.yaml, read against docs/references/grammar.md, docs/references/patterns.md, docs/question-types/question-base.md, docs/exam.md and docs/responses.md. The blanks reuse the short-answer, numeric and choice rules; earlier audits already settled those rules.

Methodology

The audit script scripts/spec_audit.py passed all 15 steps before the audit started: it compiles every Lark snippet, checks the Additional Rules tables against docs/lint-codes.md and the corpus, and checks the frontmatter tables against the schemas.

The lead auditor read docs/question-types/fill-in.md with the shared specs and recorded contradictions, ambiguities, omissions and inconsistencies. The audit focused on what is specific to fill-in: the blank references, the choice blanks, the frontmatter maps keyed by blank id, the grading strategies and the response shape. The blank rules that fill-in reuses from short-answer and numeric were checked for agreement with the decisions of the earlier audits.

One subagent turned the fill-in grammar and the shared grammar into Lark and tested what they accept and reject. A second, independent subagent scanned the fill-in document and schema, ran each complete example through mdq validate, and checked the Additional Rules table against the MUST, SHOULD and MAY requirements of the text.

Strengths and weaknesses

Strengths

  • Omissions: The worked grading table covers all four strategies with empty, wrong, partial-credit and negative blank scores, and the values agree with the formulas. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:436-457)
  • Inconsistencies: The frontmatter maps (preAccept, preReject, incorrectFeedback, domain, decimalPlaces) each show the Markdown form and the JSON form, and the rules table gives the same two codes for each: undefined-blank for an unknown id and schema-error for a blank of the wrong kind. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:51-132, 483-504)
  • Contradictions: The numeric blank rules defer to the numeric question for domain, decimal places, exact arithmetic, units and valid responses, without restating them, so the two cannot drift. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:255-260)
  • Schema mismatches: The NumericBlank answer and unit patterns of schema/fill-in.yaml match the numeric grammar (exponent of at most 3 digits, nonzero denominator) and the UNIT terminal of grammar.md, and BlankId matches SLUG. (schema/fill-in.yaml:85-90, schema/fill-in.yaml:191-211)
  • Omissions: Every complete Markdown example of fill-in.md (lines 8-19, 249-253, 267-278, 331-342) validates with mdq-py with no warning, and the arithmetic of the grading example table checks out row by row. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:441-451)
  • Inconsistencies: The frontmatter maps (preAccept, preReject, incorrectFeedback, domain, decimalPlaces) document their mapping onto blank fields with before/after YAML, and the corpus covers an undefined key and a key on the wrong kind of blank. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:51-132)
  • Grammar violations: numeric_def holds, and its two copies in fill-in.md are identical and match the numeric answer tag. Tested with Lark: accepted [^size/numeric]: 2750000 +- 10%, [^size/numeric]:42, [^length/numeric(km)]: 6400 +- 5%, [^x/numeric(m/s)]: 1, -1/3, 6.022e23 +- 0.001e23, 3.14 +- 5% +- 0.1, 3.14+-0.1, 1e05; rejected 1/0, 1e3/2, 1e1000, 01, 3,14, 1 000, 3.14 ± 0.1, an empty body, [^x/numeric (km)], [^x/numeric()], [^x/numeric/accept], [^x/Numeric]. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:193, docs/question-types/fill-in.md:236, docs/question-types/numeric.md:75-86)
  • Grammar violations: accept_def and reject_def hold, their two copies in fill-in.md are identical, and they match accept_block/reject_block of short-answer.md with the [^SLUG prefix. Tested with Lark: accepted single-line literal, exact (` date ), regex (/a/b/, /[Bb]ras[íi]lia/i), multi-line inexact, exact and regex content, lazy content after an empty tag, an empty accept tag, the /accept alias and list forms with feedback, comment and lazy lines; rejected an empty reject tag, [^planet/short-answer(km)], /short-answer/accept/reject, an unknown kind (/essay), /usr (unclosed regex), math.isnan (x) , a >` line after content or after an empty tag, a list after a blank line, and a list right after content. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:195-196, docs/question-types/fill-in.md:289-292, docs/question-types/short-answer.md:148-166)
  • Grammar violations: choice_def holds and its two copies are equivalent ("[^" SLUG "]" and blank). With list-item content as grammar.md defines it, Lark accepted the choice-blank-rich items ([*] [andes] ... with feedback and comment, [50%], [-25%]), [^capital]: with trailing ws and up to 3 spaces of indentation; it rejected a blank line before the list, text after the colon followed by a list, and the multiple-selection value [x]. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:192, docs/question-types/fill-in.md:219-220)
  • Grammar violations: The stem rule accepts the stems of the corpus: two blanks in one sentence, a stem that starts with a blank, and blanks separated by plain text; it rejects a stem without a blank, in line with the blanks minimum of one. (docs/question-types/fill-in.md:141-142)

Central risks

  • The stem of a fill-in question has no stated limits: which paragraphs form it, which blocks and inline elements may hold a blank, and which code each wrong place gives are settled only by the corpus and by the reference implementation.
  • Several fill-in rules exist only in the corpus (parse errors of blank definitions) or only in prose with two allowed behaviors, so a second implementation that follows the text can disagree with mdq-py.
  • No grading corpus exercises fill-in, so the four strategies, empty blanks and pending blanks can drift between mdq-py and mdq-js.

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:155-160

illegal. A `[^slug]` found in any of them is not a blank: implementations MUST

Description: The prose lets an implementation either reject a [^slug] in a heading, list item, table cell, blockquote or fenced code, or keep it as text. The Additional Rules table has one rule, misplaced-blank (error) for 'blanks may only appear in a plain text run of a paragraph'. The corpus uses three different outcomes: a heading gives unreferenced-blank (examples/invalid/fill-in-misplaced-blank-heading), a code span gives misplaced-blank (examples/invalid/fill-in-misplaced-blank-code-span), and fenced code is valid text (examples/valid/fill-in/blank-in-code-is-text). Impact: Two conforming implementations can give different lint codes, or one accepts a document the other rejects. The corpus fixes behavior that the spec does not state.

Suggested fix: Give one outcome per context in a table: for example, fenced code, indented code and code spans are literal text; a [^slug] in a heading, list item, table cell or blockquote of the stem, or inside emphasis or link text, is misplaced-blank. Remove 'either ... or'.

medium F22: The example table claims one set of blank scores for every strategy, but the wrong pick scores differ by strategy

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:438-439

Description: The column shows -0.5 for a wrong pick, which line 453-456 says holds only under symmetric; under the other three strategies the same pick scores 0. medium F11: For a pending fill-in, the spec does not say what score the instructor supplies

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:357-360

A response to
the question is settled only when the response to every blank is settled; if
a blank leaves its response to the instructor, the whole question is pending.

Description: docs/responses.md (Pending) says a pending response has no score until the instructor supplies one. For a fill-in, the instructor can score the pending blank (then the strategy combines it with the other blanks) or the whole question (then the strategy is bypassed). The range of a blank score from the instructor (only 0 or 1, as in a short answer blank, or any value in [0, 1]) is also not stated.

Impact: An LMS cannot tell which value to ask the instructor for, and no-mistakes gives different results for a blank score of 0.5.

Suggested fix: State that the instructor scores each pending blank, in [0, 1], and that the strategy then combines the blank scores as usual.

high F2: The spec does not say which paragraphs form the stem, so blanks in two paragraphs fail

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:137-148

stem  : inline_md? (blank inline_md?)+
blank : "[^" SLUG "]"

Description: The stem grammar describes one run of inline content. The text never says where the preamble ends and the stem begins, or whether the stem can have more than one paragraph with blanks. docs/question-types/question-base.md:132-134 only says implementations SHOULD require a one-paragraph stem. With the reference implementation, the document 'Intro.\n\nFirst [^a] paragraph.\n\nMiddle.\n\nSecond [^b] paragraph.\n\n[^a/short-answer]: x\n\n[^b/short-answer]: y' fails with unreferenced-blank for a: the first paragraph with a blank goes to the preamble, where line 178 says a [^slug] is literal text. The spec does not exercise this case and does not say it is an error.

Impact: Authors write cloze texts that span paragraphs. The result is an error that names the wrong cause, and another implementation can accept the same document.

Suggested fix: State the rule: either the stem is the last paragraph before the definitions (and a [^slug] in an earlier paragraph gives a specific error), or the stem is every block from the first paragraph with a blank to the definitions. Add an example to the corpus.

Exploitability conditions: Blanks in two or more paragraphs.

high F5: An empty blank in the response is not defined

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:403

A blank the student left empty scores 0 under every strategy.

Description: docs/responses.md:213-231 does not say how a response marks an empty blank: a missing key, a null value, or both. For a short answer blank, "" is not empty: docs/responses.md:200-204 says it is graded like any other text. The spec also does not say what an empty mapping {} is (all blanks empty, or a skip like null), nor whether a value of the wrong type for a blank (a number for a choice blank, a list for a short answer blank) is a ResponseError.

Impact: The strategies no-mistakes and all-or-nothing treat an empty blank differently from a wrong one, so the score depends on a rule the spec does not give.

Suggested fix: In docs/responses.md, Fill in the blanks: a missing key and a null value are an empty blank; {} is a response with every blank empty (the question is not skipped); a value of the wrong type is a malformed response by the rule of the blank's type. Add response cases to the grading corpus.

medium F3: The spec does not say whether blank definitions must be contiguous

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:208-210

Definitions may appear in any order, and that order need not match the order
the stem references them.

Description: The text allows any order but does not say if a paragraph can stand between two definitions. In the reference implementation, a paragraph after the first definition ends the body: a later [^b/short-answer]: y goes to the epilogue as text, and the stem reference [^b] gives undefined-blank, which names the wrong cause.

Impact: An author who puts an explanation between definitions gets an error that points at the stem, not at the definition.

Suggested fix: State that the definitions form one sequence of blocks, separated only by blank lines, and that the first other block starts the epilogue. Consider a dedicated lint for a definition tag in the epilogue.

Exploitability conditions: A paragraph between two blank definitions.

medium F6: A blank referenced more than once in the stem has no rule

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:185-187

Every blank referenced by the stem MUST be defined, and every definition MUST
be referenced by the stem.

Description: Nothing says whether the stem can reference the same id twice, as in 'The capital is [^c]. Write [^c] again.'. The response has one value per id, so two inputs would share one answer.

Impact: An implementation can render two inputs bound to one key, render one, or reject the document.

Suggested fix: State that each blank id appears at most once in the stem, with an error code (for example duplicate-blank-reference), or state that repeated references share one input.

medium F32: Stem rule allows only one inline element between blanks

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:141

Mechanical fix

stem  : inline_md? (blank inline_md?)+

Description: inline_md is defined as 'any valid inline markdown element' (singular, fill-in.md:145), and the rule allows at most one of them before, between and after blanks. Counter-example: the stem The **capital** of Brazil is [^capital]. has three inline elements before the blank (text, strong, text). The text at fill-in.md:152-176 allows markup elsewhere in the paragraph as long as the blank itself is in a plain-text run, so this stem is valid, but the rule rejects it (tested with Lark, inline_md stubbed as TEXT | STRONG | LINK).

Impact: The grammar rejects ordinary stems with any formatting, or the reader must guess that inline_md means a sequence.

Suggested fix: Use inline_md* in the rule, or say that inline_md is a sequence of inline elements.

medium F33: blank matches the text of a link [^x](url)

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:142

blank : "[^" SLUG "]"

Description: CHOICE_ID, CHOICE_MARK and VALUE_END exclude a ] followed by ( or [ because that bracket starts a markdown link. blank has no such lookahead. Counter-example: in the stem Veja [^nota](https://www.ibge.gov.br) agora., CommonMark (and markdown-it, where the footnote reference rule runs after link) reads a link with the text ^nota. The stem rule reads the blank nota followed by the text (https://www.ibge.gov.br) agora. (tested with Lark: the only parse is blank + text). The same holds for [^nota][ref].

Impact: The grammar and the markdown parser disagree on whether the stem has a blank, so an implementation that follows the grammar reports undefined-blank for a valid link.

Suggested fix: Add the lookahead of CHOICE_ID to the rule ("]" not followed by ( or [), and say in the text that [^x](url) and [^x][ref] are links.

Location: docs/question-types/fill-in.md:168-172

interpreted as an unclosed bold element `**start `, followed by a blank and then

medium F37: pattern_item includes the list marker, item does not

Location: docs/references/patterns.md:252

Description: grammar.md:73-75 says the grammar of a list item applies to the content of the item after the removal of the list marker and its padding. medium F40: LAZY_START ignores setext heading underlines

Location: docs/references/grammar.md:156

LAZY_START    : /(?!>|!(?!\[)|[*+-][ \t]|1[.)][ \t]|#{1,6}([ \t]|(?=[\r\n]))|`{3}|~{3}|(\*[ \t]*){3,}(?=[\r\n])|(-[ \t]*){3,}(?=[\r\n])|(_[ \t]*){3,}(?=[\r\n]))[^ \t\r\n]/

Description: One-line content follows the CommonMark paragraph, and a --- or === line (and also a single - or =) right after a paragraph line is a setext heading underline: the whole paragraph, tag line included, becomes a heading. The terminal reads them differently.
