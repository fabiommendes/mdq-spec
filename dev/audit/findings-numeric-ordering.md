# Audit findings: numeric and ordering examples

Scope: `examples/valid/numeric/`, `examples/valid/ordering/`. Invalid examples checked for coverage only. "impl" = mdq-py, run on hand-written probes.

All 36 `.yaml` files match what mdq-py produces, for the markdown sources and the AST-only documents. Every pinned `.lint.json` matches the mdq-py output. The mismatches below are corpus vs spec text.

### Spec problems

`ID | severity | spec reference(s) | contradiction / ambiguity / omission`

- NO-S1 | high | `numeric.md:24`, `numeric.md:110-133`, `responses.md:133-136` | Omission. No rule says `decimalPlaces` is inferred from the body, but the corpus and mdq-py infer it from the digits of a `DECIMAL` value. The field has an effect: `responses.md:135` makes "a precision that violates `decimalPlaces`" a malformed response, so `[numeric]: 3.14 +- 0.1` silently restricts responses to 2 places. Exactly N or at most N places is also undefined. In mdq-py, `domain: fraction` + body `0.75 +- 0.1` reports `ignored-decimal-places` on a field the author did not write.
- NO-S2 | medium | `numeric.md:81-97` vs corpus | The representation is MAY/SHOULD ("MAY be represented as a JSON number", "MAY be converted to a floating point number or kept as a decimal", "SHOULD be coerced"). The corpus pins one choice (`answer: 3.14` number, `"2.50"` string). An implementation that keeps `"3.14"` as a string conforms to the spec and fails the corpus. Make the rules MUST, or compare answers by value in the harness.
- NO-S3 | medium | `numeric.md:112-113`, `schema/numeric.yaml:77-90`, `lint-codes.md:23` | The domain depends on "how the value and the absolute tolerance are written", but the AST stores the tolerance as a JSON number, so `1` and `1.0` are the same. mdq-py: `[numeric]: 5 +- 1.0` gives `domain: decimal`, then reports `domain-mismatch` ("the answer 5 is written as a fraction or integer value") on a document that declares no domain.
- NO-S4 | medium | `numeric.md:70`, `numeric.md:117-124`, `schema/numeric.yaml:78` | The inference table has rows with a `fraction` abstol (`integer`+`fraction`, `fraction`+`fraction`), but the grammar allows only `INTEGER | DECIMAL` in `abstol` and the schema allows only a number. The rows are unreachable. The table also lacks `fraction`+`integer`.
- NO-S5 | medium | `responses.md:125-131` vs `responses.md:133-136` | Contradiction. A response with an incompatible unit is rejected "with grade zero" (130-131), but "a unit other than the declared one" is "a malformed response, never a wrong answer" (133-135). Line 126-127 rejects a unit when the question declares none, and says nothing about a unitless response to a question with a unit.
- NO-S6 | medium | `ordering.md:112-114`, `:173-177`, `:297-300`, `responses.md:186-187` | Contradiction on the indentation level under `fixed`. Line 175: "Two lines with the same text and the same level are the same line". Line 299: the level counts "under `indentation: "strict"`". `responses.md:186`: ignored under `fixed`. See NO-M3.
- NO-S7 | medium | `ordering.md:212-214` vs `:150-151`, `valid/ordering/reject-feedback.mdq.md` | "once a `!` comment block has started, a `>` line ends the observations" contradicts "in either order" and the grammar alternative `comment feedback?`. Read literally, the `>` block of `reject-feedback` is not feedback.
- NO-S8 | medium | `ordering.md:150-154`, `:337` | The `observations` grammar has no blank line between the two blocks, but both corpus examples use one. In the order `feedback comment` with no blank line, CommonMark reads the `!` line as a lazy continuation of the blockquote. mdq-py on `> fb` + `! cm`: `feedback: "fb ! cm"`, no comment, no diagnostic. The interleaved `! c1`, `> f1`, `! c2` also gives no error (`feedback: "f1 ! c2"`), although the rule is critical.
- NO-S9 | medium | `base.md:4-6`, `:198-202`, `ordering.md:141`, `:179-181` | Position of the epilogue. base.md: body, epilogue, then the special sections. The ordering grammar has the sections directly after the content and no epilogue. mdq-py does the reverse of base.md: an epilogue after the sections is accepted; an epilogue before `## [extra]` is `forbidden-block-element` with a wrong message ("paragraph starting with `[`").
- NO-S10 | medium | `ordering.md:165-167`, `:173-174` | Omission. How a `ul` item becomes a line is not defined for nested lists, continuation lines and items with several paragraphs. mdq-py: a nested item becomes a level-1 line; continuation lines and second paragraphs are dropped with no diagnostic (`* b` + `  continuation` gives `"b"`). "All items at level 0" is only in a comment of `brazil-timeline.yaml`.
- NO-S11 | medium | `numeric.md`, `ordering.md` (whole files), `base.md:244-245` | Omission. base.md says each question type documents how the body maps to the AST. Neither file has that section. `answer`, `tolerance.absolute`, `tolerance.relative`, `lines`, `extra`, `accept[].lines/feedback/comment` appear only in the schema and the rule tables. `numeric.md:143` defines the relative tolerance as a percentage, footnote `:188` as a fraction.
- NO-S12 | low | `ordering.md:199-206`, `schema/ordering.yaml:153-179` | Omission. No rule says the lines of an `accept`/`reject` section must come from `lines` + `extra`. A section with a foreign line can never match. mdq-py accepts it with no diagnostic. Also undefined: may an `accept` section use an `extra` line (`ordering.md:191`)?
- NO-S13 | low | `responses.md:183-184` vs `ordering.md:310-316` | A response with a distractor "is a wrong answer", but with `unmatched: manual` a response that matches no key has no score. Also undefined: a response line that is in neither `lines` nor `extra`.
- NO-S14 | low | `base.md:179-181` vs `numeric.md:62` | base.md allows "spaces around the colon". The numeric grammar has no `ws?` before `":"`. mdq-py rejects `[numeric] : 42` with "A required field is missing". The grammar has no trailing `ws?`, but the example at `numeric.md:11` ends with a space.
- NO-S15 | low | `ordering.md:146-148`, `:141` | `"##" ws? "["` allows `##[extra]`, which is not a CommonMark heading. mdq-py puts it in the epilogue as text, with no diagnostic. The grammar also requires exactly one `nl` between `[ordering]` and the block; mdq-py accepts a blank line there.
- NO-S16 | low | `schema/ordering.yaml:24`, `:60` vs `ordering.md` | The schema requires 2 lines (`minItems: 2`); the prose has no minimum. The schema gives `content` a default of `text`; the prose says it is inferred, and the AST-level case is not described.
- NO-S17 | low | `ordering.md:128`, `schema/ordering.yaml:109` | known (`lint-spec-open-decisions.md:20`) | `normalizations` MUST NOT repeat. New fact: mdq-py accepts `normalizations: [dedent, dedent]` from markdown with no diagnostic, so the schema rule is not applied either.
- NO-S18 | low | `numeric.md:104-105`, `:177-186`, `lint-codes.md:86` | `malformed-numeric-answer` is an error code, but the Additional Rules table of numeric.md has no critical row for it. The zero-denominator MUST is written only for JSON/YAML documents; in markdown, `1/0` matches the grammar and the result is undefined (mdq-py: `parse-error`).
- NO-S19 | low | `numeric.md:186`, `lint-codes.md:37` | "`decimal` or `fraction` answer" does not say if the declared domain or the written value decides. mdq-py uses the value: `answer: 1` + `domain: fraction` has no `missing-tolerance`; `answer: 0.25` + `domain: fraction` has it. Also undefined: sign and form of a string answer (mdq-py turns `+6/8` into `"6/8"`), and trailing whitespace of a code line (mdq-py keeps `"a  "`, and reads a whitespace-only line as `""` at level 0).
- NO-S20 | low | `ordering.md:244-245`, `:277-278` | `lenient` "implies the `dedent` normalization", but the AST does not list it (`lenient-indentation.yaml`). The spec should say that the implied entry is not written to `normalizations`. Related known item: indented blockquotes (`lint-spec-open-decisions.md:22`).
