title: Pororoca
---

The pororoca is a tidal bore: the sea tide pushes against the current of the
Amazon River and a wave travels upstream.

It happens mostly around the new moon and the full moon, when the tides are
strongest.

What causes the strongest tides on Earth?

* [*] The alignment of the Sun, the Moon and the Earth
* [ ] The rotation of the Earth around its axis
* [ ] The wind over the Atlantic Ocean
