The Amazon River crosses Brazil from west to east and ends in the [^ocean].

The basin holds about one fifth of the fresh water that rivers carry to the
oceans.

Its largest tributary by volume is the [^tributary], which gives the main
river a dark color in the Meeting of Waters near Manaus.

[^ocean/short-answer]: Atlantic

[^tributary/short-answer]: Rio Negro
