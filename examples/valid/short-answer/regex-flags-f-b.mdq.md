title: Bioma do Brasil central
---

Cite o bioma que cobre a maior parte do Brasil central.

[short-answer]:
* /cerrado/f
  > `f` finds the word anywhere in the response.
* /Bras/b
  > `b` matches any response that starts with `Bras`.
* /^rio/fi
  > The author's `^` is honoured even under `f`: the response must start with `rio`.
