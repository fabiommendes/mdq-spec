title: Média de uma lista
---

Sort the lines below to compute the arithmetic mean of the numbers in
`values`. Not every line belongs to the answer.

[ordering]
```python
total = 0
for x in values:
    total += x
mean = total / len(values)
```

## [extra]
```python
    total += x
    mean = total / len(values)
mean = total / x
```
