Order the events below from the earliest to the most recent.

[ordering]
* Portuguese ships commanded by Pedro Álvares Cabral reach the coast of Bahia.
* The Portuguese court flees Lisbon and settles in Rio de Janeiro.
* Brazil declares its independence from Portugal.
* The *Lei Áurea* abolishes slavery.
* Brasília is inaugurated as the federal capital.
