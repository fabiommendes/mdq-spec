Order the steps of natural selection as Darwin described them.

[ordering]
* Individuals in a population vary in the traits they inherit.
* More offspring are born than the environment can support.
* Individuals whose traits suit that environment survive and reproduce more often.
* Those traits become more common in the next generation.

## [reject]

! Inverting the last two steps is the most common mistake here: students
! read the spread of a trait as the cause of survival rather than as its
! consequence.

> A trait does not spread first and pay off later. It becomes more common
> *because* the individuals carrying it left more descendants, so the
> differential survival must come first.

* Individuals in a population vary in the traits they inherit.
* More offspring are born than the environment can support.
* Those traits become more common in the next generation.
* Individuals whose traits suit that environment survive and reproduce more often.
