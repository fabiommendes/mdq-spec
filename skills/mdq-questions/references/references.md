  Measurement in Education, 15*(3), 309–333. [haladyna]
* NBME (2021). *NBME Item-Writing Guide: Constructing Written Test Questions
  for the Health Sciences* (6th ed.). [nbme]

* Downing, S. M. (2005). The effects of violating standard item writing

  10*(2), 133–143. [downing]

* Brame, C. J. (2013). Writing good multiple choice test questions. Vanderbilt
